Print the RobotDSL Six-Axis Arm

The RobotDSL Six-Axis Arm is the RobotDSL Team's own design, drawn as CAD in code: a desk arm of six joints and a gripper on seven Feetech STS3215 (12 V, ST-3215-C018) servos, 0.57 kg in all. Stretched out level it reaches 365 mm, holding 0.1 kg there. Every piece below prints without supports, as its file lies, on a printer's bed of 250 × 210 mm, 220 mm high. You can find it in the Studio's parts library, among the attachments.

Print

  • Each file holds its piece laid on the bed as it is printed: print it as it lies, without supports.
  • PLA, printed solid (100% infill), as the robot's mass is worked out: 149 g in all.
  • A 0.4 mm nozzle: the rims round the screw heads sunk from the bed, 1.2 mm at most, are laid as three of its lines, and holes along the bed, 12 mm across at most, as arches.

Stretched out level with 0.1 kg between its fingers, its centre of mass comes 114 mm beyond the outline its feet stand in, turned the way it comes nearest: clamp its base plate to the table, a clamp each side of the base's servo, or it tips over. What drives its servos: a computer, an adapter for the servos' serial bus and a 12 V supply, which the arm does not carry; the design names no product of them.

Simulated

In MuJoCo 3.15.0, each link weighing as its solids do and each servo turning as MuJoCo Menagerie's model of the Feetech STS3215 (12 V, ST-3215-C018) has it turn (up to 2.94 N m): stretched out level with 0.1 kg between its fingers, its base clamped, its servos hold it within 0.0011 rad, the shoulder's turning 1.10 N m; each joint turned through its range at 0.5 rad/s, the others at zero, follows within 0.013 rad; unclamped, stretched out level and turned the way it comes nearest to tipping, it tips over; its gripper, closed on a box of 0.1 kg, 10 x 12 x 16 mm, whose weight then pulls it out along the fingers, holds it within 0.01 mm.

Servos

Each servo's horn is fitted as it comes: set each joint's zero in its servo, the arm straight up and the moving finger upright beside the fixed one.
