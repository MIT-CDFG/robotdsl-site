In simulation

In MuJoCo 3.15.0, each link weighing as its solids do and each servo turning as MuJoCo Menagerie's model of the Feetech STS3215 (12 V, ST-3215-C018) has it turn (up to 2.94 N m): stretched out level with 0.1 kg between its fingers, its base clamped, its servos hold it within 0.0007 rad, the shoulder's turning 0.73 N m; each joint turned through its range at 0.5 rad/s, the others at zero, follows within 0.010 rad; unclamped, stretched out level and turned the way it comes nearest to tipping, it tips over; its gripper, closed on a box of 0.1 kg, 10 x 12 x 16 mm, whose weight then pulls it out along the fingers, holds it within 0.01 mm.
