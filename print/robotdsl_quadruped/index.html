Print the RobotDSL Quadruped

The RobotDSL Quadruped is the RobotDSL Team's own design, drawn as CAD in code: a body and four legs, 2.026 kg in all. Every piece below prints without supports, as its file lies, on a printer's bed of 250 × 210 mm, 220 mm high. You can drive it in the Studio, under Walking robots.

Print

  • Each file holds its piece laid on the bed as it is printed: print it as it lies, without supports.
  • PLA, printed solid (100% infill), as the robot's mass is worked out: 617 g in all.
  • TPU for the foot: 27 g in all.
  • A 0.4 mm nozzle: the rims round the screw heads sunk from the bed, 1.2 mm at most, are laid as three of its lines, and holes along the bed, 12 mm across at most, as arches.

Between the body's plates is room for an allowance of 300 g for what a quadruped of twelve servos carries between its plates: a computer, the servos' interface and power hub, a three-cell battery and the wiring; the design names no product of them.

Servos

Each servo's horn is fitted as it comes: set each joint's zero in its servo, the leg straight out level from its hip.
