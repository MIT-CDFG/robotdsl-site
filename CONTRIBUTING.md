# Contributing to RobotDSL

RobotDSL grows through the people who use it. There are three ways to contribute.

## Tell us what breaks

In MobileCopilot Studio, **Report issue & help us improve** on a generated design sends the program, the compiler diagnostics, recent terminal lines, the render when one is available, and your note to the team. Your API key is never included. Failed cases become test cases for the next release.

Without the Studio, open an issue with the **Generated design looks wrong** form. Problems with the page or the Studio itself go in **Site or Studio problem**.

## Add a part

**Submit a part** in the Studio asks what kind of part it is and opens that kind's issue form (here: **Submit a wheel**, **Submit a motor**, and so on). It takes a product, or a part of your own: its name, a link to where it is sold or to its files (a Google Drive link is fine), its price and mass, the numbers its kind needs, how it mounts, its 3D model and the licence it is published under. A maintainer builds the part from that, checks that it compiles and mounts as described, and it appears in the Studio with your credit.

## Ask and share

Questions, designs you want to show and ideas go in [Discussions](https://github.com/MIT-CDFG/robotdsl-site/discussions).

## Keys and personal data

Never post an API key in an issue or discussion. If you posted one by mistake, revoke it with the provider first, then edit the post.

Submissions and reports are GitHub issues of this repository, so they are public: leave out what you do not want to publish, and attach files you may share.
