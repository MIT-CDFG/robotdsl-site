# Front ultrasonic open yoke — bare 3pi+ kit

Print one body. The STL is millimetres. The body is dark grey in previews; metal hardware is separate. No controller, battery cells, motors or wiring are included. This assembly is not a complete commissioned robot.

## Hardware

- Two #2-56 × 7/16 inch Phillips screws, two #2 washers, and two #2-56 nuts in the native moulded recesses. Pololu screw reference: https://www.pololu.com/product/1957 ; nut reference: https://www.pololu.com/product/1067 .
- 2 × M2.5 × 12 mm screws, M2.5 nuts and washers.

## Assembly and access

Fit the small payload nuts into the rear/underside pockets before fixing the bracket to the chassis. Seat the two chassis nuts in the source kit's hex recesses, align the actual native holes, add washers above the 5 mm feet, and tighten from above. Do not replace #2-56 hardware with M3 screws. Attach the PCB using its measured holes and the supplied edge clearance; leave connector leads free.

Two measured PCB holes provide the attachment datum; bolt preload and strength are not simulated.

## Manufacturing limits

Initial design allowances: 2.6 mm printed chassis bores, 2.4 mm M2 or 2.9 mm M2.5 payload bores, and 0.35–0.45 mm PCB edge clearance. Fit allowances need a printer/material coupon. An initial PETG print with 0.2 mm layers and four walls is a starting setup, not a qualified process. Raise the body on appropriate supports for underside pockets/overhangs; inspect and clear screw bores before assembly. Do not scale the STL to tune hole fit. No slicing or physical print has been performed.

## Scope

The fit was checked only against the bare chassis plus kit wheels; populated 2040/32U4 control boards, cells, cables, and other accessories are outside this check. No load, fatigue, acoustic response, magnetometer accuracy or complete robot electrical assessment is asserted.
