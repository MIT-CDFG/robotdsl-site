# Yahboom alloy forward ultrasonic cradle

Forward MB1010 ultrasonic sensing. Open tapered arms and a small acoustic hoop retain the actual diagonal PCB holes ahead of the nose; not a generic centre platform.

## Manufacture and hardware

Print the support in PETG, suggested 0.2 mm layers, four perimeters and at least 40% infill as an untested starting point. The cradle is base-down with build-plate supports for the acoustic hoop; keep support away from bores. Print the ToF boom base-down: its two 3 mm downward PCB pads define the lowest plane, so use removable support beneath the remainder of the base. Inspect the slicer; no sliced job or physical print is supplied. Ream only to the declared clearance sizes, and test fit without forcing the PCB.

Each support needs 2 × M3 × 12 ISO4762 screws, 4 × DIN 125 M3 washers and 2 × ISO 4032 M3 nuts through the maker's existing 2 mm top plate. These are separate purchased items. For the sonar, add 2 × M2.5 × 10 screws, 4 × M2.5 washers and 2 × M2.5 nuts. For the downward ToF, add 2 × M2 × 12 screws, 4 × M2 washers and 2 × M2 nuts. Washer thicknesses are 1.1 mm for the selected M2.5 reference, 0.7 mm for M2 and 0.5 mm for M3; choose hardware consistently with the stack.

## Installation

Use only the measured host interfaces added for the Yahboom alloy 2WD/4WD. Use the free holes, not the neighbouring occupied deck standoffs. Fit the lower washers and nuts before closing the deck. The existing electronics are not modeled, so check their actual clearance and keep service access.

The sensor's declared PCB hole positions determine the mating pose. No tape or friction-only placement is used. For the downward sensor the optics point down at the floor approximately 0.1 m away. It monitors a narrow area ahead of the right front caster; this does not provide complete left/right/rear cliff coverage, braking or safe tabletop motion.

## Limits

Physical fit, tolerances, screw torque, vibration, load qualification, acoustic response and electrical/control integration are not established.
