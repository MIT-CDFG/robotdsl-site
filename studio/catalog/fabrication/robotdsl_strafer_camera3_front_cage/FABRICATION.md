# Strafer front Camera Module 3 cage

A narrow forward cantilever with four PCB pads, lens opening and open ribbon exit.

## Parts and process

Print the individual support STL, in millimetres, in PETG or a similarly tough material. Use 0.2 mm layers, at least five perimeter lines and locally solid screw pads as a starting process; this is not a load rating. Lay the flat side/back on the build plate and support the optical cage or cantilever as required. Inspect bore size after printing.

Chassis: gobilda_strafer_chassis_kit_104mm. Use 2 M4 through bolts, washers and locking nuts. The printed seat is 4 mm thick. M4 × 14–16 mm or M3 × 12 mm is a starting stack; confirm actual chassis thickness and underside access before purchasing. Do not drill a different chassis to match this candidate.

Sensor: 4 × M2 fasteners through the exact source pattern. The support is not self-tapping. Bolts, nuts and washers are separate purchases; no hidden hardware is part of the displayed plastic.

Four M2 through bolts and nuts clamp only the PCB mounting lands. The 6 mm pad stack plus PCB and washers requires measured length selection (M2 × 10–12 mm is a starting range). Preserve the open ribbon exit; do not pinch the ribbon under the chassis foot.

## Scope

An open vendor mesh is not a watertight physical-fit certificate. Real print tolerance, strength, vibration, cable bend radii and full sensing performance remain untested. Host internals absent from the source model require inspection.

Internal fabrication, thread engagement, torque, torsional restraint and the entire robot are not certified.
