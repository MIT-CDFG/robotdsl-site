# Strafer front-right D435 fork

A wall-fastened low fork with a separate aluminium rear contact plate and accessible camera screws.

## Parts and process

Print the individual support STL, in millimetres, in PETG or a similarly tough material. Use 0.2 mm layers, at least five perimeter lines and locally solid screw pads as a starting process; this is not a load rating. Lay the flat side/back on the build plate and support the optical cage or cantilever as required. Inspect bore size after printing.

Chassis: gobilda_strafer_chassis_kit_104mm. Use 4 M4 through bolts, washers and locking nuts. The printed seat is 4 mm thick. M4 × 14–16 mm or M3 × 12 mm is a starting stack; confirm actual chassis thickness and underside access before purchasing. Do not drill a different chassis to match this candidate.

Sensor: 2 × M3 fasteners through the exact source pattern. The support is not self-tapping. Bolts, nuts and washers are separate purchases; no hidden hardware is part of the displayed plastic.

Machine the separate contact pad from 3 mm aluminium. Two M3 screws traverse the 5 mm printed seat plus 3 mm pad and then enter the camera threads. Select length for the vendor thread-depth limit; the metal plate is not a printed substitute.

## Scope

An open vendor mesh is not a watertight physical-fit certificate. Real print tolerance, strength, vibration, cable bend radii and full sensing performance remain untested. Host internals absent from the source model require inspection.

Internal fabrication, thread engagement, torque, torsional restraint and the entire robot are not certified.
