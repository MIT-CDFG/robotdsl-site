# ROSbot downward D405 inspection arm

A roof-to-nose bracket puts the close-range camera over the front edge rather than looking through the roof.

## Parts and process

Print the individual support STL, in millimetres, in PETG or a similarly tough material. Use 0.2 mm layers, at least five perimeter lines and locally solid screw pads as a starting process; this is not a load rating. Lay the flat side/back on the build plate and support the optical cage or cantilever as required. Inspect bore size after printing.

Chassis: husarion_rosbot_xl_mecanum_chassis. Use 2 M4 through bolts, washers and locking nuts. The printed seat is 4 mm thick. M4 × 14–16 mm or M3 × 12 mm is a starting stack; confirm actual chassis thickness and underside access before purchasing. Do not drill a different chassis to match this candidate.

Sensor: 1 × 1/4-20 UNC fasteners through the exact source pattern. The support is not self-tapping. Bolts, nuts and washers are separate purchases; no hidden hardware is part of the displayed plastic.

Use a 1/4-20 tripod screw through the 6 mm seat. Camera insertion must not exceed 5 mm. A nominal 10 mm under-head length gives about 4 mm insertion before washers; measure it. External cheeks limit gross rotation, but this single screw and unqualified cheek contact do not establish torsional retention. Recheck orientation after tightening. The near-field optical axis points down beyond the nose.

## Scope

An open vendor mesh is not a watertight physical-fit certificate. Real print tolerance, strength, vibration, cable bend radii and full sensing performance remain untested. Host internals absent from the source model require inspection.

Internal fabrication, thread engagement, torque, torsional restraint and the entire robot are not certified.
