# Romi front MB1010 acoustic yoke

A compact annular yoke matches the actual diagonal PCB holes and clears the front caster.

## Parts and process

Print the individual support STL, in millimetres, in PETG or a similarly tough material. Use 0.2 mm layers, at least five perimeter lines and locally solid screw pads as a starting process; this is not a load rating. Lay the flat side/back on the build plate and support the optical cage or cantilever as required. Inspect bore size after printing.

Chassis: pololu_romi_chassis_kit. Use 2 M3 through bolts, washers and locking nuts. The printed seat is 4 mm thick. M4 × 14–16 mm or M3 × 12 mm is a starting stack; confirm actual chassis thickness and underside access before purchasing. Do not drill a different chassis to match this candidate.

Sensor: 2 × M2.5 fasteners through the exact source pattern. The support is not self-tapping. Bolts, nuts and washers are separate purchases; no hidden hardware is part of the displayed plastic.

Two M2.5 through bolts and nuts use the diagonal board holes. The 8 mm pad stack plus the board and nut generally needs 14 mm under-head length; measure the real stack. The circular centre stays open for the rear electronics and transducer. Use insulating washers against the PCB.

## Scope and evidence

An open vendor mesh is not a watertight physical-fit certificate. Real print tolerance, strength, vibration, cable bend radii and full sensing performance remain untested. Host internals absent from the source model require inspection.
